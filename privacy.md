# Privacy Policy — My rclone

Last updated: November 8, 2026

## Purpose

My rclone is a personal Google OAuth application used with
rclone to access Google Drive files for my thesis research.
It is not offered as a service to other users.

## Google Drive access

The application requests Google Drive permissions through
Google's authorization process.

Depending on the permissions granted, rclone can list and
download files, and may also upload, modify, or delete files.
I use this access only for my own research workflow.

## Data storage and sharing

Files accessed or downloaded through rclone are stored in
the locations I select for my research.

This application does not sell Google user data or share
it with advertisers.

No Google Drive files, passwords, OAuth client secrets,
or authentication tokens are published on this website.

## Credentials

Authentication tokens are stored in the rclone configuration
on the computer or server where I use rclone. These credentials
must be kept private.

## Revoking access

I can revoke this application's access through my Google
Account's third-party connections settings.
